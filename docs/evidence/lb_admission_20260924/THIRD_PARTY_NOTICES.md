## Changes to admitted audio

Original files are unchanged. A deterministic 15-second crop was decoded to mono 16 kHz, peak-capped only when necessary, and encoded as PCM16 WAV. Crop offsets, gain, quantization scale and both original and derived hashes are recorded per file. Attribute the source corpus and this transformation when sharing derivatives. The CC BY 4.0 terms continue to apply; no endorsement is implied.

## Excluded sources

Jamendo originals retain their individual recorded CC BY/BY-SA terms. They are excluded from this delivery's training audio because the separate Jamendo TDM authorization condition remains unresolved. This does not invalidate the recorded CC licenses.
MLAAD is restricted to noncommercial academic research. The user confirmed competition model development as the purpose. New MLAAD recordings are excluded in this run because the additional academic-research scope is not established by that statement alone. NC is not treated as a blanket competition ban. No new license acceptance or external rights request was made.

These decisions apply to the newly collected candidates, not a fresh rights audit or ownership claim over all historical rows in the combined bank.
