Commercial deployment, sale or commercial licensing is not cleared. Preserve attribution, this license and source links when providing clips for verification.

The 146 audio files are deterministic 15-second crops, decoded/resampled to mono 16 kHz and converted to PCM16, with peak attenuation when needed. Per-file original and derived hashes, crop offset, gain and labels are retained in roles-v6/manifest.jsonl. Original recordings were not overwritten. Unknown music labels are masked.
