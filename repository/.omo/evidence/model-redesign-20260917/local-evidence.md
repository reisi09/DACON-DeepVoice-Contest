## Decision summary

The local evidence supports preserving a frozen MERT330 stem branch as a control, improving evaluation coverage and the training distribution before a wholesale architecture replacement, and testing component-level mixed/sequential supervision. It does **not** support “raw is useless,” “the architecture has failed,” or “mixture training alone is sufficient.” The latest music heads never trained on the newly generated mixtures; the direct File heads did. All latest official totals remain `null`, and local component gains have previously failed to improve the submitted score.

## Contradictions and ranked implications

1. **P0 — Make the selection target identifiable.** All latest totals are null; natural music vocals lack voice labels, and historical music-only wins have not established leaderboard wins. Obtain or construct correctly labeled component panels covering real/fake speech, singing and accompaniment; keep source-disjoint external holdouts with complete official labels. Report component metrics and uncertainty until that exists.
2. **P0 — Retire reused data from final confirmation.** Preserve it for controlled diagnostics, but preregister a new parent/artist/speaker/generator-held evaluation. Source grouping within old data prevents direct training leakage for a new head, not researcher adaptation from many earlier comparisons. Deduplicate and use parent-level bootstrap or repeated source splits; never count derivatives as independent sources.
3. **P1 — Fix exposure/coverage before declaring architecture failure.** Frozen M330 music heads were trained only on natural music. A matched ablation adding known-component simultaneous and sequential examples is warranted; telephone/codec/stereo and4–60s duration strata must be represented. Do not assume this alone fixes missing singing-voice labels, source shortcuts, file aggregation or OOD generators.
4. **P1 — Keep stem/max as a deployable control and raw as a research branch.** Latest validation rejects the chosen global blend/direct head, not all raw representations or fusion. Raw seed variability and File-head participation contradict “raw useless.” A conditional or learned fusion needs a predefined, adequately powered evaluation; do not introduce it solely to rescue mixed test results.
5. **P1 — Increase independent real music coverage, not synthetic example count.** Latest natural test has8 real FMA tracks; S1/S2 has12. Cap NSynth contribution and control duration/generator mix, but preserve it as a separate instrument-domain stress test. A model can exploit source artifacts even after NSynth removal.
6. **P2 — Treat fullFT/separator results as hypotheses.** S2 EAT outperforms MERT on the same natural test, while S0 fullFT underperforms frozen MERT. These are conditional results under different training exclusions, data sizes and in some cases batches. Separator adaptation has actual gradients and one historical improvement, yet pseudo-target agreement is not stem truth.
