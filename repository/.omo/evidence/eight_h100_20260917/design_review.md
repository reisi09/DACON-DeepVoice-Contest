# Eight-H100 experiment design review

Review scope: `.omo/plans/eight-h100-20260917.md`, the current canonical and fresh manifests, retained caches/results, and `.omo/ulw-research/20260917-audio-five-years/SYNTHESIS.md`. This is a pre-launch design review. Implementation may continue, but the H100 run should not start until the bounded amendments below are reflected in the data receipt/config/result contract.

## Contradictions

- **Blanket causal-result requirement conflicts with acknowledged exposure and confounding.** The plan acknowledges limited independent speech sources, historical evaluation reuse, and source confounding (`.omo/plans/eight-h100-20260917.md:14,27`), yet requires every result to contain a “causal comparison” (`:33`). Fresh FMA is REAL-only and fresh SONICS is FAKE-only, while all canonical rows and the named fresh cohorts have already participated in project diagnostics. **Amendment:** replace the required field with `estimand`, `comparison_scope`, and `claim_limit`. Reserve causal language for a single controlled intervention on matched parents; call source-confounded, historically exposed, and constructed-panel results diagnostic.

## Ambiguity

- **“Two fresh-source cohorts”:** the checkout has three relevant manifests: `results/six_h100_20260917/fresh_fma_manifest.csv`, `fresh_sonics_manifest.csv`, and `fresh_speech_manifest.csv`. The executor must not guess which one is omitted. **Amendment:** list every included manifest and SHA-256 in config/data receipt; include canonical Echoes/FMA and the fresh FMA, SONICS, and speech rows; exclude NSynth as stated.
- **E1 “linear/MLP reference versus balanced sampler”:** this can change head architecture and sampling simultaneously. **Amendment:** choose one fixed head for the sampler comparison. If both linear and MLP run, use a declared 2x2 design or report architecture and sampler effects separately.
- **E1 “source x generator x parent balanced”:** no exact probability distribution, replacement rule, or empty-generator handling is defined. **Amendment:** balance within label and task-eligible kind, then uniformly sample source-generator strata and connected components inside each stratum; keep a fixed 16 REAL/16 FAKE batch, fixed update count, and identical seed-specific draws across paired arms.
- **E4 “fixed dev-selected fusion”:** the fusion form, candidate set, and selection objective are unspecified. **Amendment:** predeclare the fusion candidates and component-level calibration objective before extraction; use the same small head and same input view for spectrum-only, MERT-only, and fusion. Keep raw-spectrum as a separate diagnostic if the primary MERT input is the accompaniment stem.
- **E8 “diagonal shrinkage Gaussian or distance baseline”:** `or` leaves the method, shrinkage, score, and operating threshold to the executor. **Amendment:** freeze one formula before test access, fit normalization and density on train REAL only, define high distance as FAKE, and set the operational threshold from calibration only.

## Missing Constraints

- **Speech-role allocation:** the canonical speech pool has exactly six recorded speaker components (480 rows, balanced REAL/FAKE within speaker) and fresh speech adds four nonoverlapping speaker components (32 rows). A generic 8:1:1 split cannot guarantee three calibration speakers. **Accepted amendment:** allocate 3 train / 3 calibration / 2 dev / 2 test speakers; put one fresh speaker in each role and allocate historical speakers 2/2/1/1. The two-speaker test is diagnostic and cannot support a speaker-population CI.
- **Split-before-mix invariant:** “prefer distinct background” is too weak for independence (`.omo/plans/eight-h100-20260917.md:27`). **Amendment:** assign every source connected component to a role first, construct only within role, union all contributor atoms into each child `group_id`, reject any contributor crossing roles, and use a distinct background component for each vocal series unless the resulting component collapse is explicitly reported.
- **Label-mask contract:** canonical preparation has 1,216 unknown `VOICE_FAKE`, 480 unknown `MUSIC_FAKE`, and 1,116 unknown `VOICE_PRESENT` values (`_workspace/redesign_20260917/input-preparation/prepare.log:202-207`). **Amendment:** every loss and metric declares its eligible mask and denominator. CE uses only known target labels; unknowns remain unknown. File, Voice, Music, and presence outputs are never inferred from another field unless construction makes that truth explicit.
- **Budget and seed semantics:** global `head_steps=500`, `lora_steps=200`, and seeds are insufficient to prove matched exposure. **Amendment:** persist per arm and seed the batch label counts, component counts, sampled IDs, updates, optimizer schedule, and augmentations. Use seeds 41/42/43 for stochastic learned heads and E7. Do not manufacture seed variation for deterministic probes, convex fits, or the closed-form density model.
- **Bootstrap estimand:** “group bootstrap” does not say whether uncertainty includes fitting and selection. **Amendment:** resample whole provenance-connected components and retain all variants/windows and paired arm predictions. Call the resulting interval conditional on the locked fitted models. For calibration-selection stability, optionally rerun the selection rule over precomputed candidate predictions inside each calibration resample. Never bootstrap rows, windows, crops, or seeds as independent observations; emit no population CI for one-component slices.
- **Historical exposure register:** the M330 full-data model used prior train/dev/sealed with no independent final holdout (`EXPERIMENTS_ALL_2026-09-17.md:92`), and the canonical bundle is explicitly historical/diagnostic (`_workspace/redesign_20260917/input-preparation/README.md:29-36`). **Amendment:** add `prior_project_exposure` and `baseline_role` per cohort/model. Retrain all causal heads on the new train role. M330 full1206 and existing `predictions_M330` may be shown only as exposed comparators; the frozen encoder has unknown pretraining overlap.
- **E7 teacher definition:** the teacher checkpoint, logit convention, temperature, and retention population are absent. **Amendment:** name and hash the exact AntiDeepfake teacher; cache eval-mode logits on the identical waveform/view/window; use Bernoulli/logit-consistent KL at the declared temperature; keep labels authoritative; and report teacher-label conflict only on label-known rows.

## Execution Risks

- **Reusing old prepared splits would silently remove Voice evaluation.** The retained prepared validation/sealed manifests contain no speech (`_workspace/redesign_20260917/input-preparation/README.md:34-36`; `prepare.log:20-23,30-33,90-93`). **Fix:** rebuild a merged source manifest and the accepted 3/3/2/2 speaker allocation; do not reuse the old `prepared/{train,validation,sealed}.csv` role assignments.
- **Fresh music can reward corpus recognition.** `fresh_fma_manifest.csv` is 64 REAL rows; `fresh_sonics_manifest.csv` is 40 FAKE rows. Their receipts explicitly retain corpus/pretraining uncertainty and unreviewed voice labels (`fresh_fma_receipt.json:123-138`; `fresh_sonics_receipt.json`). **Fix:** evaluate source-confounded FMA-v-SONICS stress separately. Use historical Echoes/FMA matched-parent structure for within-panel music comparisons and never call the fresh contrast generator robustness.
- **Derived panels can collapse to one independent unit.** The prior 568-row pilot has 93 connected components, while each eight-row dev/test `mix_minus6dB` slice is one component (`.omo/ulw-research/20260917-audio-five-years/verify-connected-groups.json:2-23`). **Fix:** audit connected-component counts after all construction, before feature extraction; reject cross-role overlap and withhold CI/robustness claims for collapsed slices.
- **Cache reuse can import the wrong view, layer, or exposed head.** Existing cache evidence covers final representations; E6 needs new intermediate layers, and previous M330 predictions came from exposed fitted heads. **Fix:** bind cache identity to derived-audio SHA-256, every parent SHA-256, model hash, raw/stem view, layer, sample rate, window/hop/tail/padding, and code hash. Reuse encoder features only when all keys match; never reuse prior fitted predictions as new-split outputs.

## Topology Gaps

- **E1 — FAIL until amended:** sampler effect is not identifiable while `linear/MLP` and sampling are both free. Hold head, class exposure, updates, augmentations, split, and seed-specific draws fixed; separate any architecture comparison. Report fresh source-confounded strata apart from matched historical strata.
- **E2 — CONDITIONAL PASS:** pure-noise masks are exact: `MUSIC_PRESENT=0`, and File/Voice follow the target voice. FMA has `MUSIC_FAKE=0`; overlaid target voice makes `VOICE_PRESENT=1` and `VOICE_FAKE` follows the target, but background vocal presence remains metadata-unreviewed. SONICS has `MUSIC_FAKE=1` and `FILE_FAKE=1`, but all 40 selected rows have `no_vocal=False`; for target-real mixtures `VOICE_FAKE` remains unknown, while target-fake mixtures can truthfully set it to 1. Define the round trip as original vocal -> mixed waveform -> recovered vocal, with identical gain/windows, and treat raw-mixture versus recovered-vocal change as a separator diagnostic. Do not claim verified isolated backing.
- **E3 — CONDITIONAL PASS:** use aligned clean/telephone/MP3 views from the same parent and same window; apply transforms to both classes. CE-only and CE+consistency must receive identical labeled examples, class exposure, updates, and augmentation draws. Define whether label-free consistency is allowed on target-unknown rows; if used, disclose that population separately. Opus+telephone remains evaluation-only.
- **E4 — FAIL until fusion is frozen:** use the same input view and same head family for spectrum-only, MERT-only, and fusion, then lock the fusion selection rule on calibration. Source-confounded FMA-v-SONICS performance is a stress result, not evidence that the residual detects synthesis.
- **E5 — CONDITIONAL PASS:** every partial FAKE and matched REAL-to-REAL control must share base parent, position, length, gain, boundary/fade, and window schedule; union the inserted contributor into provenance. Prefer same-speaker/content-matched REAL/FAKE inserts where available so a source discontinuity is not mistaken for a fake boundary. Durations, positions, occupancies, and windows are repeated measures, not bootstrap units. The output is a constructed-boundary/pooling diagnostic, not natural partial-fake robustness.
- **E6 — CONDITIONAL PASS:** MERT layer selection is a music/accompaniment experiment and Anti layer selection is a speech experiment. Select a layer only within each family/population using the declared calibration metric; use identical heads, budgets, masks, and seeds. Do not rank MERT layers against Anti layers or pool their denominators.
- **E7 — FAIL until teacher and pairing are frozen:** compare frozen+classifier, q/v-LoRA+classifier CE, and the same LoRA CE+0.1 KL with identical seed-specific initialization, minibatch IDs, augmentations, class balance, updates, and optimizer schedule. Verify nonzero adapter gradients, changed/reloadable adapters, and unchanged frozen weights. The teacher never overwrites labels, and no exposed test-trained head supplies KL targets.
- **E8 — FAIL until method/threshold are frozen:** fit only train REAL, use a predeclared high-distance-is-FAKE score, and lock any threshold on calibration. Report shifted-REAL FPR by source and FAKE recall separately. Because fresh REAL and fresh FAKE come from different corpora, do not claim one-class generator robustness from their pooled contrast.

## Verdict

**GAPS FOUND - 25 issues above must be resolved in the data/config/result contract before H100 launch.** All eight experiments remain executable as bounded diagnostics; none requires human listening as a launch blocker. Metadata labels remain unreviewed where recorded, label masks must stay honest, and no fresh-holdout, singing, natural-partial-fake, robustness, causal-generalization, or official-score claim is supported by this run.
