================================================================================
Supplementary Material: Legal and Ethical Considerations
for the HAIM (Human-AI Music) Dataset
================================================================================


1. Overview of Data Distribution

The HAIM dataset contains music from both open-source AI models and commercial
AI music platforms. Due to differences in licensing, Terms of Service, and
copyright implications, the dataset is distributed in two forms:

  (a) Audio files included:
      - ACE-Step 1.5 (6,000 tracks) — open-source model, locally generated
      - MusicGen (5,000 tracks) — open-source model (Meta), locally generated
      - Lyria Pro 3 (6,000 tracks) — generated via Google DeepMind API
      - MTG-Jamendo (6,000 tracks) — Creative Commons licensed human music
      - All B-category hybrid tracks (32,040 tracks) — derived works
      - All C-category mix-set tracks (12,000 tracks) — derived works

      These tracks are either generated locally using open-source models,
      sourced from CC-licensed collections, or are derived works created
      through our own processing pipelines. We are able to distribute the
      audio directly.

  (b) Metadata only (JSON with platform URLs):
      - Suno (20,000 tracks) — commercial platform, scraped
      - Udio (9,344 tracks) — commercial platform, scraped
      - Mureka (7,212 tracks) — commercial platform, scraped
      - B5: AI Vocal + Human Track (2,040 tracks) — YouTube AI vocal covers

      For these sources, we provide structured JSON/CSV files containing
      track IDs, platform URLs, version information, and other metadata.
      Audio files are not included. Researchers may independently retrieve
      the audio using the provided URLs for their own non-commercial use.


2. On Terms of Service of Commercial Platforms

The Terms of Service of Suno, Udio, and Mureka each contain provisions that
restrict or prohibit automated scraping:

  - Suno (User Conduct section) prohibits "data mining, robots, scraping,
    or similar data gathering or extraction methods."
  - Udio (Section 5.2) prohibits the use of "robot, spider, crawlers,
    scraper, or other automatic device" to access or extract data.
  - Mureka's terms similarly restrict automated collection of content.

We acknowledge that our data collection methods involved systematic crawling
of publicly available content from these platforms, which may be inconsistent
with these ToS provisions. However, we emphasize:

  (1) ToS violation does not constitute illegality. Terms of Service define
      what a service provider deems appropriate use, not what is legal or
      illegal under applicable law.

  (2) Our use is strictly non-commercial and academic in nature. We do not
      redistribute the audio content from these platforms. We provide only
      metadata (track IDs and URLs) that point to publicly accessible pages.

  (3) The metadata we release cannot be used to reconstruct or substitute
      for the original service. It serves only as a reference for
      reproducible academic research.


3. On Copyright

AI-generated music collected from commercial platforms may incorporate
copyrighted material through user prompts, training data, or platform
processing. Several considerations apply:

  (a) Upstream infringement risk: If platform users provided copyrighted
      lyrics or other protected content as input, the resulting outputs may
      carry infringement risk. All three platforms (Suno, Udio, Mureka)
      require users to ensure their inputs do not infringe third-party
      rights, while disclaiming liability for user-generated content.

  (b) Our research use: Our analysis of this content falls under academic
      research exceptions. In particular:
      - EU Directive 2019/790, Article 3 permits Text and Data Mining (TDM)
        for scientific research purposes, even when the source material is
        copyrighted.
      - 17 U.S. Code Section 107 (Fair Use) supports transformative,
        non-commercial uses for research and education.

  (c) Data release form: For commercial platform content, we release only
      metadata and URLs — not the audio itself. This approach avoids direct
      redistribution of potentially copyrighted material while maintaining
      research reproducibility.

  (d) Use of copyrighted lyrics (B6): The B6 subcategory contains AI-
      generated audio conditioned on human-written lyrics sourced from
      the K-Pop Lyric Dataset. Song lyrics are copyrighted literary works
      under both Korean Copyright Act and international conventions (Berne
      Convention). We include the generated audio files because:
      - The audio is a machine-generated output, not a human performance
        of the lyrics. The copyright status of AI outputs conditioned on
        copyrighted prompts remains legally unsettled.
      - Our use is strictly non-commercial and for academic research into
        AI music detection, not for music consumption or distribution.
      - The generated audio is transformative in nature — it serves as a
        research artifact for studying the boundary between human and AI
        authorship, not as a substitute for the original songs.
      - EU Directive 2019/790 Article 3 (TDM exception) and 17 U.S.C.
        Section 107 (Fair Use) support this research use.
      Nonetheless, we acknowledge that the original lyrics remain the
      intellectual property of their respective authors and rights holders.
      The lyrics themselves are not distributed in this dataset; only the
      AI-generated audio derived from them is included.

  (e) Open-source and CC-licensed content: The locally generated tracks
      (ACE-Step, MusicGen) are produced by us using open-source models
      under their respective licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0). The human music
      baseline (MTG-Jamendo) is distributed under Creative Commons licenses.
      These can be freely shared for academic purposes.


4. On Locally Generated and Derived Content

A significant portion of the HAIM dataset consists of content we generated
or processed ourselves:

  - A2 (open-source): Generated locally using ACE-Step 1.5 and MusicGen
    on our own hardware. These outputs are original to our research.

  - A2 (Lyria Pro 3): Generated via the Google DeepMind API. Audio is
    included as the API terms permit research use of generated outputs.

  - B-category (hybrid): Created by applying mastering, editing, variation,
    inpainting, and vocal replacement processes to either open-source AI
    outputs or CC-licensed human music. These are transformative derived
    works produced through our own pipelines.

  - C-category (mix-set): Created by temporally combining segments of
    human (MTG-Jamendo) and AI (ACE-Step) music. These are synthetic
    compositions designed specifically for AI music detection research.

For all derived works, source attribution is preserved through metadata
files (metadata.jsonl, JSON sidecars) that record the origin of each
component.


5. On Data Privacy

  - No personally identifiable information (PII) is included in the dataset.
  - User identifiers from commercial platforms have been removed from all
    metadata. Only track-level identifiers (platform song IDs) are retained
    for reproducibility.
  - The MTG-Jamendo subset uses only the track IDs from the original dataset,
    which do not contain artist PII beyond what is already publicly available
    in the MTG-Jamendo distribution.


6. Intended Use and Restrictions

The HAIM dataset is released exclusively for non-commercial academic research
purposes, including but not limited to:

  - AI-generated music detection and classification
  - Analysis of AI music generation quality and characteristics
  - Study of hybrid human-AI music production workflows
  - Evaluation of temporal AI content detection in mixed compositions
    
The dataset must NOT be used for:

  - Commercial purposes or competitive analysis of AI music platforms
  - Training commercial AI music generation systems
  - Redistribution of audio content from commercial platforms
  - Any purpose that would violate the intellectual property rights of
    original content creators


7. Reproducibility

To ensure full reproducibility of our dataset:

  - For audio-included tracks: Files are provided directly and can be used
    as-is for research.
  - For metadata-only tracks: Researchers can retrieve audio using the URLs
    in the JSON files. We note that platform content may be removed or
    modified over time, which is an inherent limitation of this approach.
  - Generation scripts for all locally produced content (ACE-Step, MusicGen,
    hybrid processing, mix-set creation) are included in the scripts/
    directory with full parameter documentation.
